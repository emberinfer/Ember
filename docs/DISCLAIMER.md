# Disclaimer

Ember is experimental, open-source research software provided **as-is, without
warranty**, subject to the [Apache-2.0 license](../LICENSE).

## Testnet Spark

**Testnet Spark is valueless test credit.** It is not money or a financial
instrument and is not offered for sale. The Ember project operates no exchange
or fiat on/off-ramp of any kind. Spark balances, stake, holds, transfers, and
settlement on the testnet exercise network accounting using this test credit.

## Participation and local rules

Nothing in this documentation is an offer, solicitation, or investment advice.
Participation as an operator or consumer is at your own risk and subject to your
own local rules.

The network is open to independent operators contributing inference capacity.
This does not establish independent validator governance: current testnet
validators are operated by a single organization, validator admission is static,
and gateway account custody is centralized. There is no third-party security
audit, production SLA, or public mainnet.

Signed evidence provides attribution; numerical audits are probabilistic and do
not prove every computation correct. Gateways, executors, and auditors see the
plaintext needed for their roles. Review the [status and limitations](../README.md#status-and-limitations),
[public network trust model](PUBLIC_NETWORK.md#trust-and-authority), and
[threat model summary](THREAT_MODEL_SUMMARY.md) before participating.
